Slow-Fired Traditions That Refuse to Hurry.

At Donthanpalle, machines do not rush time. Fire, wood, stone, and generational memory govern each earthen vessel. Our khansamas begin at dawn, grounding native spices in basalt stone mortars so volatile essential oils retain their royal aroma.

Charcoal Hearth & Sealed Copper Degs

Heavy lid seams sealed with wet wheat dough to recirculate steam inside the meat without a single drop of added water.

02

Basalt Mortar Hand-Pounded Masalas
